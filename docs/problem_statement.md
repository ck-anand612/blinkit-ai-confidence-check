# Blinkit Confidence Experience

## Problem Statement Document — Part 4 (AI-Native MVP)

---

## Background

Blinkit has successfully established itself as India's leading quick-commerce platform for grocery delivery. As the grocery business matures, Blinkit is expanding into high-growth non-grocery categories such as Beauty & Personal Care, Electronics, Home Essentials, and Baby Care.

Among these, Beauty & Personal Care represents a large market opportunity due to its high purchase frequency, premium product mix, and repeat purchase potential.

However, unlike grocery purchases, beauty products are high-consideration purchases where customers seek confidence before buying.

Despite an expanding Beauty & Personal Care assortment, adoption remains lower than expected. User research suggests that increasing product availability alone is not sufficient to drive adoption — purchase hesitation is the key barrier.

---

## Business Goal

Increase adoption and conversion of Beauty & Personal Care products by reducing customer hesitation during the purchase journey.

**Success Metric:** Increase Beauty & Personal Care conversion rate while reducing customer drop-offs caused by external research.

---

## User Research

**Research Conducted**
- 34 survey responses
- 10 user interviews

**Key Findings**

1. **Awareness is not the problem.** Most users already know that Blinkit sells Beauty & Personal Care products.
2. **Users seek additional product validation before completing a purchase.** They commonly rely on ChatGPT, Reddit, Google, YouTube, Nykaa, and Amazon to validate authenticity, suitability, or value before buying.
3. **The biggest concerns are:**
   - Authenticity of products
   - Trust in product quality
   - Whether the product suits their needs
   - Return and replacement confidence
4. **Users associate Blinkit with convenience and speed, but not with informed beauty purchases.**

---

## Root Cause Analysis

Research indicates users do not leave Blinkit because products are unavailable. Users lack sufficient purchase confidence before committing to a high-consideration Beauty & Personal Care purchase — this introduces friction into what is otherwise a fast, convenient shopping experience.

**The core problem is not product discovery. It is insufficient purchase confidence at the moment of decision.**

---

## Problem Statement

Users trust Blinkit for grocery shopping but lack sufficient confidence to make high-consideration Beauty & Personal Care purchases. This confidence gap increases decision friction and delays or discourages purchase completion.

---

## Core Insight

Quick-commerce users optimize for speed, not research. They do not necessarily need more information — they need enough reassurance to confidently buy now.

---

## Opportunity

Blinkit already excels at helping users purchase everyday essentials quickly. By extending this strength to high-consideration categories through a confidence-first buying experience, Blinkit can reduce purchase hesitation without compromising the speed and convenience that define quick commerce.

---

## Product Principle

**Engineer Purchase Confidence, not Product Discovery.**

Instead of helping users spend more time researching products, Blinkit should help users make confident purchase decisions within seconds.

---

## Why Blinkit?

The proposed solution leverages Blinkit's existing competitive strengths instead of introducing a generic AI assistant. Blinkit's advantages include:

- Existing customer trust built through frequent grocery purchases
- Rich first-party customer purchase history
- Operational reliability and quick-commerce infrastructure
- First-party inventory and fulfillment
- Fast fulfillment and customer support capabilities

Rather than competing on AI alone, Blinkit combines these structural strengths with AI to create a differentiated purchase confidence experience that competitors cannot easily replicate.

---

## Success Metrics

**Primary Metrics**
- Increase in Beauty & Personal Care conversion rate
- Reduction in product page exits
- Increase in Add-to-Cart rate
- Reduction in external research behavior (self-reported / proxy measure)

**Secondary Metrics**
- AI Confidence Check usage rate
- AI Confidence Check engagement and completion rate
- Repeat Beauty & Personal Care purchases within 30 days
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Average Order Value (AOV)

---

## Scope

**Included**
- Beauty & Personal Care products
- Product Detail Page experience
- AI Confidence Check (concern-driven summary generation)
- Authenticity Assurance
- Trust Signals
- Local Confidence Signals
- Risk-Free Purchase Assurance

**Out of Scope**
- Shopping chatbot
- Personalized/algorithmic recommendations
- Checkout redesign
- Loyalty program
- Inventory optimization
- Search improvements
- Cart experience
- Delivery optimization

---

## One-Line Summary

Blinkit should transform its product pages from information pages into confidence experiences that reduce purchase hesitation and help users make informed purchase decisions in seconds.
